About

Visual App Launcher shows your Mac apps as a visual grid of icons, so you can launch what you need by recognition instead of typing a query.

Open it with Option-Space, use search when you need it, and browse Home, Favorites, and All Apps. Recently used apps stay close at hand in Continue.

Create your own sections and freely move apps between them to organize your library around your workflow. You can also add application folders, adjust columns and icon size, and hide apps that you do not want to show in the launcher. Hidden apps are protected before they can be restored.

The app runs locally on your Mac and does not display ads.
