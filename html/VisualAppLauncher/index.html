About

Visual App Launcher gives your Mac a clear visual library for browsing, organizing, and opening applications. Switch between compact cards and an icon grid, then launch the app you recognize without having to type a query.

Press Option-Space to show or hide the library. Home keeps recent activity and frequently opened apps close at hand, while Favorites and All Apps provide quick access to saved tools and the complete catalog. Search works across the entire library, and apps can be sorted by name, recent use, most used, or newest.

Start with Work, Create, Develop, and Utilities, or build your own sections. Drag apps into Favorites or sections, reorder sections in the sidebar, and use their context menus to rename them, choose an icon, or delete them. You can also add read-only application folders, adjust icon size and sidebar appearance, and hide apps you do not want in the library. Restoring hidden apps requires Mac authentication.

Your library preferences stay on your Mac. The app scans only standard application folders and the additional read-only folders you choose, automatically refreshes when apps change, and includes no ads or third-party analytics.
