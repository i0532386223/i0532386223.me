01 / Why it exists

An investigation that stays readable.

Follow a subsystem, compare related streams, and narrow a long capture without losing the original record. Search and filtering are separate, so finding one message does not have to hide the context that explains it.

01

Capture in parallel

Give each predicate its own named stream. Start and stop streams independently while saved records remain available.

02

Find the relevant moment

Combine text conditions, exclusions, and time ranges. Find navigates saved history and preserves the surrounding records.

03

Inspect and share

Read complete messages in the inspector and export text or NDJSON with a manifest for further analysis.

04 / Your data

Privacy,
in plain language.

Read the privacy policy ↗

  • Saved captures

    Captured records and stream definitions are kept locally. Logs can contain process names, paths, identifiers, and message text.

  • System privacy

    The app shows the fields that macOS makes available. It cannot reveal private fields that the operating system has redacted.

  • Sharing

    Exports contain captured data. Review and redact sensitive records before sending them to another person.
