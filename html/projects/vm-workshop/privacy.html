Privacy policy

Data handling for the application and this product website. Updated 6 November 2026.

Virtual-machine storage

Disks, settings, snapshots, and saved memory are stored in local VM bundles. A bundle may contain all data from the guest system.

Shared resources

Network, clipboard, and shared folders are separate controls. Enable the resources your experiment needs.

Transfers

Restore image downloads contact Apple. Guest networking, shared files, and exports can move data outside a VM when you enable them.

Support requests

If you contact the developer, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to answer your request. Do not send passwords, recovery keys, private documents, medical records, or complete diagnostic archives unless a specific, appropriate transfer has been agreed.

This website

This website serves its fonts, screenshots, and scripts from the same site. It has no analytics or advertising scripts. App Store links, email links, and other external links open only when you choose them. The main design pages store your animation preference in this browser.

Your choices

You control the app’s local files, settings, and exports. Copies you share or place in another service must be managed there. For data or privacy questions, contact i0532386223@gmail.com.
