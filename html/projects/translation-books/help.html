View on the App Store ↗

A simple starting point

  1. Choose a book. Open a book from your library or import your own EPUB.
  2. Tap for meaning. Select a word or sentence to see its translation.
  3. Keep the rhythm. Listen to the original text and return to your reading position.

Common questions

Which books can I use?

Import supported EPUB books and text that you are entitled to use. The App Store listing describes the formats in the released version.

Where can I get it?

Translation Books is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
