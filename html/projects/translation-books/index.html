01 / Why it exists

More reading. Less switching apps.

Choose a book you want to read, tap a word or sentence, and continue with its meaning in view. The library and reading position stay close to the reading experience, instead of turning every page into a trip to another app.

01

Read your own books

Bring EPUB books and supported text into a personal library, then return to your saved reading position.

02

Translate in place

Choose a word or sentence without leaving the reader. Keep the surrounding text close to its meaning.

03

Listen as you read

Hear the original text with speech support and adjust the reader to suit a longer session.

04 / Your data

Privacy,
in plain language.

Read the privacy policy ↗

  • Your library

    Books, settings, and reading progress are stored on your device. They are not sent to a developer-operated book server.

  • Services

    The app’s published privacy policy describes its current translation and local-data practices. Review it together with the version you install.

  • Support

    Only information you choose to send in a support email reaches the developer. Do not attach private or copyrighted book files.
