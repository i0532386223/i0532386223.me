A simple starting point

  1. Add an address. Choose a server or website you are entitled to check.
  2. Review availability. Run a check from your device.
  3. Return when needed. Keep your chosen list ready for another look.

Common questions

Does the developer run my checks?

No. The app connects from your device to the destinations you select.

Where can I get it?

Use its App Store page to see current device and regional availability.
