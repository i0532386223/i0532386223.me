04 / Your data

Privacy,
in plain language.

Read the privacy policy ↗

  • Your list

    Server and site addresses and settings are stored on your device.

  • Direct connections

    Checks contact the addresses you enter. Those destination services receive normal network request data.

  • Developer collection

    The app’s existing privacy policy states that monitoring results and your configured list are not sent to the developer.
