01 / Why it exists

Less to keep in your head.

Keep the plan you have agreed with your clinician, record what actually happened, and see what supply remains. Your cabinet, intake journal, and label photos give you a record you can return to.

01

A cabinet in your pocket

Keep names, strengths, label photos, and supply together. Scan a label to reduce typing, then review every field.

02

A record to return to

Log an intake now or add an earlier time. Correct entries and leave a note when a detail matters.

03

Small shortcuts

Group medicines in intake bundles. Each medicine keeps its own record and supply count.

Medicine Notes is a personal record tool. It does not prescribe, choose doses, check interactions, or replace a doctor or pharmacist.

04 / Your data

Privacy,
in plain language.

Read the privacy policy ↗

  • On your device

    This version stores the journal locally, has no app account or cloud sync, and excludes its database from automatic device backup.

  • Optional online guide

    MedlinePlus search contacts the U.S. National Library of Medicine only when you choose to search. It does not attach your journal or label photos.

  • Your exports

    JSON exports are not encrypted. Keep them private. The full policy explains local deletion, older development copies, and support requests.
