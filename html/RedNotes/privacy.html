Privacy policy

Red Notes for macOS, iOS, and iPadOS. Updated November 25, 2026.

Notes on your device

You can write and back up notes on your device without a cloud account. Red Notes stores its ZIP backups locally until you copy or share them. The app keeps preferences on your device and uses elapsed time for local operations. It does not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps or websites.

Optional cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional. Sign in with Apple provides an account identifier and, when shared, a verified email address, including an Apple relay address. The Red Notes service uses Cloudflare to store the account, access grants, sessions, and the libraries and revisions you choose to sync. These can contain note text, folder names, photos, audio, videos, documents, and location information you added. Ordinary synced notes are readable by the service. A password-protected folder uses client-side encryption; some sync metadata, including its public root name, remains visible. The service uses your account and storage usage to control access and enforce your storage allowance.

Mac dictation

On Mac, dictation requests microphone and speech recognition access when you start it. Red Notes uses on-device recognition when Apple supports the selected language. Otherwise Apple may process the audio to return your transcript.

Optional assistant

If you choose to use the assistant, Red Notes sends your prompt and the context you select to the configured OpenAI API service using your API credentials. The assistant settings identify the connection. Your credentials are protected by the device Keychain. Sending content to the assistant is separate from cloud sync.

Delete your cloud account

In Settings, open Cloud Sync and choose Delete Cloud Account to remove your account and its cloud data. Confirm the request while signed in. The service ends active sessions and queues deletion of cloud notes, attachments, permissions, and its Apple authorization. It retries unfinished cleanup automatically. Local notes and backup ZIP files remain under your control. You can remove those separately. If an older account has no saved authorization token, the app explains how to remove Red Notes from Sign in with Apple in your Apple Account settings.

Support messages

When you contact Ivan Kramarchuk, your email address and message are used to answer your request. The support form sends your message through Formspree and uses Cloudflare Turnstile for spam protection when you open the form. See the website privacy policy for contact data, retention and provider details.
