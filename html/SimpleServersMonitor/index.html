About

You can easily check the availability of your servers and sites. You can add server and site lists in plain text.

The main difference from similar applications is the ability to add and change the list of servers as text. In this application you can add tens and hundreds of servers and sites at once by simple copying. Also in the application is a comfortable, beautiful and modern design.
